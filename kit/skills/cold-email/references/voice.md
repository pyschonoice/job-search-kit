## How that maps to email
Keep: <traits to keep>. Tone it down: <slang, lowercase "i", rambling>. An email can have **one** small personal line, not three.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Start with them: something specific they built or said. | "I came across the <role> opening at <Company> and would love to be considered." |
| One concrete thing the user built, with its real number. | A keyword list: "I've worked extensively with X, Y, Z." |
| Say why *this* company, tied to the user's work. | "...particularly interests me, and I'd be excited to contribute." |
| A clear, small ask. | "Would be happy to connect and discuss the opportunity." |
| Contractions: I'm, I've, it's. | "I believe my experience aligns well with what <Company> is building." |

## Words and phrases
**Use:** built, shipped, runs, cut, broke, fixed, figured out, curious, tried, noticed, I'd like to, would you be open to.

**Never:** "I hope this email finds you well", "I am writing to express my interest", "passionate", "leverage", "synergy", "aligns well", "excited to contribute", "esteemed", "kindly", "do the needful".

## Shape
- **Greeting:** "Hi <First name>," (never "Dear", never "Hello," with no name).
- **Paragraphs:** 1-2 sentences each, a blank line between them. It must read easily on a phone.
- **No bullet lists** in a first email.
- **Sign-off:** the standard signature block below.

## Signature (every email, always)
Every email (first emails, follow-ups, recruiter notes) ends with exactly this block, nothing after it:

```
Thank You,
<Full Name>
<phone>
<email>

resume - <resume link>
linkedin - <url>
github - <url>
```

`<resume link>` is a public share link (Google Drive "anyone with the link") of the resume that fits the email. List them here, one per resume variant:
- **<Variant A>** (<which companies it suits>): <link>
- **<Variant B>** (<which companies it suits>): <link>

The link replaces attachments: no PDF attachments, no "my resume is attached" line.
